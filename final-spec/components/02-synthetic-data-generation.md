# Synthetic data generation

Version 4.0 (amended 2026-10-05) | Implementation baseline | Component GEN

## Requirements and boundary

Generate profiles -> base purchases/delivery/honest returns -> propensities/relationships/campaigns -> abuse-layer campaign orders -> compatible abusive actions -> immutable snapshot -> private labels. Reconstruct customer/transaction generation; retain supplied 300-SKU/37-style catalog with a named adapter. Do not claim reconstruction reproduces unavailable original code. Scalar shared_device_count is not an identity relationship.

Snapshot 2026-09-09T23:59:59+05:30; inclusive history 2025-09-10..2026-09-09; Asia/Kolkata; INR; 10,000 fresh profiles per full world. Opaque customer/relationship IDs are world-namespaced. Catalog fixed; defect/target truth redraws. No orders before signup or SKU launch. Current active_flag does not imply historical retirement.

Profiles are targets, not model fields. Established personas (Dormant, Occasional, Seasonal, Loyalist, High_Intensity) have tenures up to 1,095 days, so most accounts predate the history window; persona order targets are annual rates, scaled by the share of the account's yearly order-date weights that falls after its signup, so an account's daily rate does not depend on when it joined (High_Intensity is an established high-volume, high-return persona, no longer a short-tenure burst), capped at one order per two eligible days, and no account places more than one order on the same day. Use profile-config.yaml; Fraud_Proxy becomes label-neutral High_Intensity. Emit account_status=unknown. Honest return propensity is independent of abuse. Intersect festive windows with signup/history; no eligible festive date means realized festive share zero. Handle year-crossing windows. Observed order gap uses consecutive dates, null for fewer than two orders; target_days_per_order is only an intensity input. Optional clustering is exploratory.

Joint overlays: m_i=persona multiplier for positive-target accounts, otherwise zero; M=mean(m) across all accounts; w_i=m_i/M. Draw one category: claim-only .035w, temporary-only .025w, both .005w, neither 1-.065w. Expected whole-world rates are 4% claim/3% temporary/.5% overlap, not exact quotas. Invalid probabilities fail; no clipping. Multipliers 1 Occasional/Seasonal/Loyalist, 1.5 New_Growth, 2 High_Intensity. Stress 2507 sets actionable multipliers to 1. All-dormant boundary fixtures explicitly disable overlays.

Reserve private units (order_id,order_line_no,unit_index). Generate honest events first; reserve all honestly requested units, including withdrawn requests. Each positive-propensity account draws one private action_rate per enabled family from the action-rate law of the generator-config block in force, keyed per account and family: Beta(1.2,4.8), mean .20, under generator-config 1.3 and again under 4.0 (AM-51); Beta(1.2,7.2), mean .143, under 3.0 only. Solo opportunities are drawn at that rate per relevant base-layer delivered order. A relevant order has a unit compatible with the family before reservations; report blocked opportunities after reservations. Any delivered unit is compatible with temporary use, but its opportunity probability is clip(action_rate*m,0,1) with m=2.5 when the order has an unreserved delivered occasion-wear unit (that unit is then used) and m=0.55 otherwise, so occasion wear is favoured while the mean stays near the account rate. Choose compatible unreserved units; competing families use keyed random winner with no forced replacement. Disabling abuse preserves base sales and the content of every honest request and removes every campaign order; return header identifiers, line numbers and derived totals may differ. Purpose/entity-keyed random streams prevent one phase advancing another.

Actions: deceptive_nonreceipt_claim requires actual receipt and knowingly false nonreceipt; deceptive_contents_claim knowingly false wrong/missing/empty content; temporary_use_return actual use beyond fitting followed by return. Intent/condition stay private. Submitted rejected/late abuse counts; unsubmitted, future, withdrawn honest requests or group membership alone do not. Abusive requests never withdraw. y_abuse=1 iff a completed action is on a delivered line with requested_at<=cutoff; maturity not required. Label every account; rank eligible only.

Households: 12% members, groups 2-5. Coordination groups: 40% of any-abuse-propensity accounts, sizes 3-6. With probability .20 per order of a household member, another member who has already signed up orders the same style 0-2 days later (a household co-order: base layer, privately marked, never followed by another). A co-order delivered to the same address as the order it follows is genuinely not received with probability .80 when that order is. Type-sharing probabilities per group independently: household address/device/payment 1/.7/.3; abuse .4/.8/.7. Per-member p=U(.6,.9) households or U(.7,1) groups; independent use per order/type. If overlapping memberships offer the same type, keyed equal-choice between offered values; report actual sharing. Add 1-2% address-only shared-location members in groups 26-60. No graph edge from high-fanout address alone.

Campaigns: each world draws a private hot list of 4 target styles without replacement, weighted by median catalog unit price, among styles with >=1 SKU launched by history_start. Each coordination group draws 1-2 hot-list styles (price-weighted) and 2-4 campaign dates uniform over the history window, of which only those on or after its start (the signup of its second claim-capable member, or of its only one) are kept, so a ring that starts late runs fewer campaigns. A member takes part only in campaigns on or after signing up, and a ring without claim-capable members plans nothing. Each date is assigned one of the group's styles uniformly. Only deceptive claim types coordinate, so only claim-capable members take part; each participates per date with probability .60. A participant first reuses a compatible unreserved unit of that style delivered in the 14 days up to the campaign date; otherwise the participant places a campaign order on the first day from campaign_date+U{0,1,2} on which the account has no other order: an ordinary basket in which one line, at a uniform random position, is a uniform launched SKU of the style, with ordinary quantities, pricing, discount, shipping, identifier-usage and fulfilment rules. One unit of that line is claimed. A deceptive claim's reason code is drawn with the mix honest claims show per order (item_not_received .27, wrong_item_sent .29, missing_parts .29, empty_package .15) and is a non-receipt claim exactly when the code is item_not_received. A deceptive non-receipt claim on a solo opportunity or a campaign order covers the whole parcel with probability .35, as 35% of genuine non-receipt claims on multi-line orders do: every unreserved delivered unit of the order is claimed, each further sale line as a companion action with its own claim delay, and companions never count towards style targeting truth. Every transaction claim, genuine or deceptive, solo or coordinated, is requested min(30, round(Gamma(shape 2, scale 3 days))) days after delivery, so claim timing cannot reveal intent.

Campaign orders are abuse-layer events. Private order_truth marks them and no operational field does. Their unclaimed units take honest events like any delivered unit; they draw no solo opportunities and take IDs from a separate keyed stream so base IDs never shift. An attempt that is pre-signup, pre-launch, after cutoff, cancelled, lost, undelivered by cutoff or whose claim falls after cutoff stays uncompleted with a recorded reason; there are no retries and no quotas. planned_targeted_by differs from completed_targeted_by. A style is targeted iff >=1 group has >=3 distinct accounts with completed coordinated claims on it visible at cutoff; fewer is sub_threshold coordination, reported separately. Truth never depends on detector thresholds or +/-7-day matching.

Ordinary logistic return probability incorporates preference, fit, bracketing, latent quality and noise; initial category range .10-.30. Mark exactly round(8% of styles), at least one, defect-prone at random per world, then sample units; not every exposed buyer returns. Benign heavy-return overlay 6%, honest .28-.48 range; power users (8% of Loyalists) affect volume, not gate membership. Genuine delivered-line claims: nonreceipt .5%, wrong item .4%, missing parts .4%, empty .2%; carrier damage .5% independently. Each delivery address has a private keyed multiplier g=Z^2 (Z standard normal; mean 1, variance 2) applied to all four genuine claim rates for lines delivered there (probability clipped at 1), so honest repeat claimants cluster at a few addresses while population rates stay near the stated values and independent of abuse propensity. Carrier damage is not address-scaled. Whole parcels can also go missing together: a delivery incident occurs with probability .00375 per address and 14-day block of delivery dates, each order delivered there in the block then fails with probability .8, and every unit of a failed order is genuinely not received. The independent non-receipt rate is .2% so total non-receipt stays near .5%; the address multiplier applies to the independent component only. Resolve multiple honest causes once per unit, retain latent causes privately. Temporary-use reasons 85% fit/discretionary and 15% false poor_quality/not_as_described. Return timing under generator-config 3.0 (AM-49): an honest item return is requested at round(Beta(2,5)*window) days, except that it follows the late law Beta(5,2) with probability .5 for an account that is a late returner (a private habit of 12% of active accounts, independent of abuse) and .05 for any other account; a temporary-use return follows Beta(5,2), except that with probability .3 it follows the honest law Beta(2,5). The late law of honest returns is the temporary-use law, so the timing of a single late return does not reveal intent, and about one honest item return in twenty falls in the last quarter of its window (one in two hundred before 3.0). Each new draw comes from its own keyed stream. In worlds generated before 3.0 honest returns always followed Beta(2,5) and temporary-use returns always Beta(5,2).

generator-config.yaml specifies numeric operational settings and explicitly identified consolidation defaults. These are simulation assumptions, not Indian market statistics. Freeze expanded config/lock before accepted generation. Change only on development worlds with dated rationale before validation. Never resample sealed worlds for attractive results. Order channel is mobile_app .60/web .30/marketplace .10, independent of all other draws; loyalty_tier and marketing_channel emit unknown; reason_text and disposition emit null. Difficulty calibration (component 05) may change only the action-rate Beta parameters, the address multiplier dispersion and the genuine claim rates.

Development/demo scenarios: five sale states; genuine and deceptive claim codes; linked/unlinked households; low-link abuse; high-fanout addresses; heavy returns; defective buyers; honest late returners; bracketers; low-volume abusers; mixed cases; nonacting group members; campaign orders and reused purchases; sub-threshold groups; one-off abusers; honest repeat claimants at high-failure addresses. Deterministic boundary fixtures supplement rare random coverage, never alter sealed worlds. No pending inspection expansion.

## Inputs, outputs and dependencies

- Inputs: Bundled catalog/profile/festival inputs, seed and generation config.
- Outputs: Seven operational tables/manifest plus isolated truth and diagnostics.
- Dependencies: Data contracts, validation and experiment contracts.

## Acceptance criteria

| ID | Pass condition and required evidence |
|---|---|
| GEN-AC-01 | Pinned seed/config/lock reproduces canonical sorted tables; different seeds vary; CSV/Parquet values match. |
| GEN-AC-02 | Adapter preserves 300 SKUs/37 styles, prices/windows/variants/launch dates and source hash. |
| GEN-AC-03 | Abuse-off preserves base sales and honest request content and removes every campaign order (return header identifiers and line numbers may differ); quantity-three fixture with one honest/one abusive request yields two requested units. |
| GEN-AC-04 | Five sale states and all cutoff chronology pass; future plans do not enter snapshot. |
| GEN-AC-05 | Population-average probabilities equal .04/.03/.005; invalid allocation fails; no forced realized quotas. |
| GEN-AC-06 | Labels reconstruct from action/request/unit joins; nonacting group members and planned-only targets are not positive; a style is targeted iff one group has >=3 accounts with completed coordinated claims. |
| GEN-AC-07 | Assigned sharing and realized links differ legitimately; one-order usage may be 0%/100%. |
| GEN-AC-08 | Coverage, eligibility, excluded abuse, segment support and blocked-campaign reports precede full generation. |
| GEN-AC-09 | Campaign orders obey signup/launch/cutoff chronology and are marked only in private order_truth; coordinated claims occur only on world hot-list styles; uncompleted attempts keep a reason. |
| GEN-AC-10 | Per-account action rates and per-address genuine-failure multipliers reproduce from keyed streams; realized means versus .20 and the configured claim rates are reported; the temporary-use occasion weighting matches a fixture; claim timing, basket shape, header line order and same-day orders do not separate honest from abusive requests. |

Acceptance status is recorded per criterion in acceptance-register.csv (status, evidence, date, reviewer). Prose requirements are not implementation evidence.
