Three things to take away

The queue

Built from what customers return and when, not from how much. On these worlds that triples the share of reviews that find something and cuts honest heavy returners in the queue from 44 percent to under 5.

The case

Every case shows its denominators, its expectation and the record behind each link, so a second reviewer can disagree with the first on the page.

The next step

Everything here is simulated. The same pipeline on a real month of returns, with the same Evaluation page to say what changed.

Words to use: selected for review, evidence, pattern in the record, expectation, denominator, draft memo, simulated world. Words to avoid: fraudster, fraud score, confidence, proof, guilty.
