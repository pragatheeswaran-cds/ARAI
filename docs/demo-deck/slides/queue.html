The queue a reviewer sees, column by column

Rank and score

A percentile against the training worlds, never a probability. Threshold this run: 95.5.

Tier

Anchored to the budget, not to fixed bands. High always means “in this period’s queue”.

Gate outcome

A stated presentation rule, not a model output. Escalate 98: in a ring or wardrobing elevated. Hold 186: the rest of the queue.

Primary signals

Chips with denominators: “Two or more late returns, 4 of 13 item-return lines”.

Return-rate rule

The rule’s own rank for the same customer, and whether it would have selected them.

Status

New, in review, awaiting approval, approved, rejected. Who opened it and when.
