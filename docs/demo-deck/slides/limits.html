Limits, read from the page

Two signals carry the model

Transaction claims and late returns. Out of fold, precision is 77 percent with all features, 64 without claims, 61 without timing, 38 without both. How sharply they separate is a setting of this simulation.

Some abusers cannot be ranked

168 of 1,758 abusers on the validation worlds, and 165 of 1,773 on the test worlds, have fewer than three mature sale lines and are outside the ranking.

One generator, one world at a time

Results show stability across simulated worlds, not performance on real returns. Honest repeat claimants are reviewed more often by the model than by the rule, and the gate has a leg for them.
