# Exploration record, 2026-10-02

Exploration of the development worlds under specification v1.2 (component 05). Source:
`notebooks/02_exploratory_data_analysis.ipynb`, with tables under `reports/eda/2026-10-02/`.

Worlds: fixtures 2001-2003 (1,000 customers), training 2101-2110 (10,000 customers) and stress 2501-2507.
All twenty validate against the data contract. **No validation, test or demo world has been generated.**
Every number below comes from simulated data and says nothing about real customers.

## Changes made before these worlds were generated

Both were agreed by the owner on 2026-10-02 and are recorded as AM-14 and AM-15 in `final-spec/DECISIONS.md`.

1. **Longer tenures.** Dormant, Occasional, Seasonal and Loyalist tenures now reach 1,095 days. About 57% of
   accounts predate the history window, the festive peaks are visible again, and eligibility rose from 56.6% to
   59.3%.
2. **Ring campaigns start at ring formation.** Campaign dates fall between the latest signup among the ring's
   claim-capable members and the snapshot. Attempts that failed because a member had not signed up went from
   164 of 303 in the earlier scratch world to none; 2,120 of 2,901 attempts now complete across the ten training
   worlds.

Three wording clarifications went with them (AM-16): power users are 8% of Loyalists, exactly three styles are
defect-prone per world, and order targets are capped at one order per eligible day. The all-dormant boundary
world and the quantity-three boundary fixture are now covered by tests.

- **Generation targets are met.** Claim, temporary-use and joint propensity are 4.0%, 3.1% and 0.6%; mean
  action rate 0.198; genuine fulfilment failures 1.5% of delivered units; carrier damage 0.5%; cancellations
  3.0%. Two measures sit below target for a known reason: the late share of honest item returns is 1.6%
  against 2% because late requests near the cutoff fall after it, and the heavy-return overlay is 5.5% of all
  accounts against 6% because Dormant accounts cannot receive it.
- **The return-rate rule is inside its 20-30% calibration band** without tuning.
- **The difficulty levers work.** Each world has 431-493 honest customers with two or more genuine claims
  against 366-402 abusers (both counted over all customers, not only eligible ones), and a rule on claim
  count reaches only 41.7% among eligible customers. In world 2101, the 4.3% of delivered
  units that went to addresses with a multiplier of 4 or more carry 26% of all genuine claims.
- **Timing separates temporary use, not claims.** Temporary-use requests arrive at a mean 0.71 of the window
  against 0.27 for honest requests. Solo deceptive claims follow the same timing law as honest claims (0 to 10
  days). **Correction after independent review:** ring claims do not. They follow the specification's Gamma
  delay of up to 30 days, so every claim made more than 10 days after delivery is a ring claim. The pooled
  mean of 0.14 hid this; see the review section below.
- **Reason codes do not give intent away.** Every code used deceptively is also used honestly in every world;
  the highest deceptive share is 37% (`item_not_received`).
- **Evaluation segments are large enough.** Over five training worlds the benign segments hold 2,591 heavy
  returners, 5,747 defective-product buyers and 3,157 household members, far above the 200 the gate requires.
- **Households and rings both create links.** 54% of household members and 69% of ring members have a supported
  neighbour; no ungrouped account does. In world 2101, ring members are a quarter of all linked accounts.
- **Stress worlds move the intended measure.** Halving and doubling the action rate gives 760 and 2,127
  completed actions against a training mean of 1,384; doubling households doubles the household segment;
  doubling defect incidence gives six defect-prone styles. In world 2507, with all multipliers at 1, propensity
  does not depend on persona (chi-square 0.24 on 4 degrees of freedom, p = 0.99); in a normal world it does.

## Decision taken from this evidence

**AM-17: the style-targeting threshold is 0.05, down from 0.10.** Over 370 style-worlds, the 40 targeted styles
have a linked-claim share between 0.028 and 0.348 (median 0.165); the other 330 never exceed 0.034. With at
least three linked claimants required, precision is 1.0 at every threshold tried and recall is 0.825 at 0.10,
0.85 at 0.075 and 0.925 at 0.05. The quality threshold stays at 0.30: all 30 defect-prone style-worlds lie
between 0.369 and 0.448 and no ordinary style exceeds 0.039, so 0.30 detects all of them and the original 0.50
would detect none. Both thresholds were set on training worlds and are still to be reported, not tuned, on
validation and test worlds.

## Points to watch

1. **Shortcuts.** On held-out training worlds, persona alone gives an AUC of 0.62 over all customers, above the
   0.60 level at which the specification asks for an investigation. The cause is accounts with no orders, which
   can never be abusers: among eligible customers persona alone gives 0.54 and order count alone 0.56.
   Prevalence still rises with order count, from 1.9% at 1-2 orders to 6.9% at 21 or more, as the specification
   expects.
2. **Missing evidence is informative.** 79% of honest eligible customers have no transaction claim at all,
   against 25% of abusers. A missing-value indicator on claim features will carry signal of its own.
3. **Recent orders dominate.** Short-tenure accounts place their whole order target within their tenure, so
   weekly orders still roughly triple between February and the snapshot, and 17% of delivered lines are not
   yet mature.
4. **No sub-threshold styles at full scale.** Every hot-list style in the training worlds ended up targeted, so
   the sub-threshold truth level only occurs in the 1,000-customer fixtures.
5. **The fan-out cap rarely binds.** At most three address values per training world exceed 25 accounts and
   none in the fixtures.
6. **Honest repeat claimants may be too many to be realistic.** About 220 honest customers per world have three
   or more genuine fulfilment failures in a year. This is what makes claim counting weak, and it is a lever the
   calibration step may change.

## Independent review (same day)

An independent critic (a separate agent on the Fable model, with no access to this record's reasoning beyond
the files) reviewed the exploration and the data. Its full report is in
`records/2026-10-02-eda-critic-review.md`. It found the data structurally clean but not yet fit to build the
model story on, and it corrected two statements above. Its main findings, with the ones re-checked here on
worlds 2101, 2105 and 2110 marked as confirmed:

1. Ring claims use a different latency law from every other claim: all claims more than 10 days after
   delivery are ring claims (confirmed: 20-30 such claims per world, none honest).
2. Campaign orders are always one line of one unit, so about 98% of ring claims sit on single-line orders
   against about 33% of honest claims (confirmed).
3. Abusive lines are numbered after honest lines inside a mixed return header, so a line that is out of time
   order is always abusive (confirmed: 23-36 lines per world, none honest).
4. Ring activity is concentrated in the last 90 days of the window, a side effect of the ring-formation rule
   and short tenures (not re-checked).
5. Order volume still triples through the year and about 9% of orders are a second or later order by the same
   customer on the same day (the same-day figure is confirmed).
6. The link signal has no honest noise: almost no honest linked customers claim on the same style within a
   week, so the targeting result shows the three-claimant rule working, not the 0.05 share (not re-checked).
7. A first logistic model reaches about 0.60-0.69 precision in the top 5%, below the 0.75-0.85 target, and the
   household leg of the gate is at risk (the critic's own approximation; no model exists in this repository).

None of these is fixed yet. Items 1 to 3 are artefacts a model could exploit instead of behaviour and should be
resolved before features are built.
