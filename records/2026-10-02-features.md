# Feature engineering record, 2026-10-02

Implementation of specification component 06 (v1.3) and the exploration of the engineered features.
Source: `src/arai/features.py`, `src/arai/dataset.py`, `notebooks/03_feature_engineering_and_eda.ipynb`,
tables under `reports/features/2026-10-02/`. Simulated data only.

## What exists

- **18 model features** in the order of `contracts/features.yaml`, one row per customer and world, computed from
  the seven operational tables only. Identifiers are used for equality alone.
- **Relationship graph**: an edge needs two shared identifier types; a value shared by more than 25 accounts is
  dropped; an empty graph is valid.
- **Style and SKU evidence**: quality and targeting signals with the six states.
- **Wardrobing indicator**: integer points, a band only with a timing signal, independent of the model score.
- **Frozen training reference**: category return rates (the fallback for a category without peers) and the
  request-timing baseline. Everything else is an aggregate of the snapshot being scored.
- Features are frozen per world under `data/artifacts/features/`; labels are joined only afterwards.
- 17 tests on hand-calculated fixtures cover the two-level expectation, partial quantities, a sole buyer, a
  category without peers, a zero variance, the eligibility boundary, graph edges and the fan-out cap, linked
  claims at 7 and 8 days, the six style states and the wardrobing boundaries.

A world of 10,000 customers takes about 4 seconds. In all 20 development worlds no category lacked peers, so
no model feature depends on the frozen reference and cross-validation folds can share the feature files.

## What the features show (ten training worlds, 53,024 eligible customers, 2,876 abusers)

| Feature alone | AUC | Range across worlds |
|---|---|---|
| requested_refund_ratio | 0.79 | 0.77-0.81 |
| residual_z | 0.79 | 0.77-0.80 |
| raw_return_rate | 0.79 | 0.78-0.80 |
| claim_share (where defined) | 0.78 | 0.77-0.80 |
| max_identifier_fanout | 0.71 | 0.69-0.73 |
| supported_neighbor_count | 0.66 | 0.63-0.68 |
| mature_sale_lines / log_order_count | 0.57 | 0.55-0.59 |

1. **The product-adjusted residual is no better alone than the raw return rate** (both 0.79). The expected
   rate barely varies between customers (0.14-0.33), so it corrects for defective styles but cannot tell an
   honest heavy returner from an abuser: heavy returners average a residual of 2.9, above every abuser group.
   Defective-product buyers do come down, from a raw rate of 0.22 to a residual of 0.09.
2. **Claims and links carry the difference.** Solo and ring claimants have a claim share of 0.55-0.61 against
   0.07-0.11 for every honest group. Heavy returners look exactly like honest customers on claim share. This is
   what a model can use that the return-rate rule cannot.
3. **Missing evidence is itself evidence.** 20% of honest eligible customers have no return request at all,
   against none of the abusers; `coordinated_claim_share` is undefined for 80% of honest customers and 25% of
   abusers. The missing-value indicators will carry real weight in any model.
4. **Shared identifiers are strong.** The median abuser has an identifier shared by three accounts; the median
   honest customer by one. Rings are 40% of abuse-prone accounts by design, and their members act often.
5. **Volume is a weak shortcut** (0.57), and recency features carry almost nothing (open_window_share 0.53,
   trend_90d 0.49).
6. **Features are stable across worlds.** Means differ by a few percent between worlds, more for rare signals.

## The wardrobing indicator is weak

| Band | Flagged | Really temporary use | Precision | Recall |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| elevated | 598 | 143 | 0.24 | 0.13 |
| elevated or possible | 1,678 | 280 | 0.17 | 0.25 |

Of 1,109 eligible customers with a temporary-use action, 829 show no elevated signal. The indicator works on
customer-level averages (mean timing score, median window ratio), and a customer who wardrobes a few items among
many ordinary returns does not move those averages. It also alerts on 18% of honest customers who have any
late-in-window request. The specification makes this indicator report-only, so it does not block anything, but
as it stands it should not be presented as a working capability. A version built on the share or count of late
requests would be the obvious next step and would need a dated decision.

## Points to watch for training

- Three of the four strongest features are near-duplicates (rank correlation 0.87-0.95 between raw rate, residual
  and refund ratio).
- The gate's household leg will be tested by the graph features: households also create links.
- All of this is measured on the worlds the generator was explored on, with features designed in knowledge of
  the generator.
